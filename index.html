APPLY WHAT YOU LEARNED

Three practical application sections

01 · Everyday conversation practice

Start a three-minute conversation with a friend or family member. Open politely, ask two open questions, listen without interrupting and close naturally.

  • Topic: a favourite place, film or weekend plan
  • Use: “Tell me more”, “What happened next?” and “That sounds interesting.”
  • Goal: balance speaking and listening

02 · Phone call and message practice

Role-play a phone call to book or change an appointment. Then write a short WhatsApp message confirming the date and time.

  • Start with a greeting and your name
  • Explain the purpose clearly
  • Repeat important details to confirm
  • Close with a polite thank-you

03 · Explain, respond and reflect

Record a two-minute explanation of a real event or a simple process. Then practise one respectful I-statement for a situation where you need to express a concern or say no.

  • Organise your explanation: first, then, finally
  • Use calm, clear and respectful language
  • Listen to your recording and note one strength and one improvement
